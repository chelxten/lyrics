Find the songs you need, then print them or put them on the screen.

Song library Search and read every song. Song sheet Print the songs you picked, or save them as a PDF or JPG. PowerPoint One verse per slide, full screen, for a projector or TV. Subtitles One or two lines at the bottom of the screen, for video and live streams.

How it works

  1. Pick your songs. In the song library, tap + next to each song you want. You can also search for songs right on the song sheet, PowerPoint and subtitles pages.
  2. Choose what to make. A song sheet to print, PowerPoint slides for a projector or TV, or subtitles for a video or live stream.
  3. Save or share it. Print it, download it, or copy a link to send to others.
